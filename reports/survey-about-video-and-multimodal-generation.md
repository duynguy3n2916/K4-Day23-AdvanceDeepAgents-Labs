# Survey about Video and Multimodal Generation
## TL;DR
- Recent advancements in video generation highlight frameworks like FreeMatching which enhance video coherence and control across transformations [1].
- Modern multimodal generation techniques integrate multiple modalities for coherent content generation, utilizing diffusion models alongside large language models [2].
- Major challenges remain in achieving temporal consistency and synchronizing different modalities, especially in long video contexts [3].

## Background
The field of video and multimodal generation has seen significant progress due to advancements in machine learning techniques. Recent frameworks focus on generating high-quality video content by integrating various models, addressing existing limitations [1][4]. The need for improved synchronization and coherence across modalities continues to drive research efforts as tasks become increasingly complex.

## Advancements in Video Generation
Recent frameworks like FreeMatching and CausalDreamer show promise in enhancing video generation, allowing for a seamless merging of generative and semantic representations, which improves coherence and quality in generated videos [1][4]. Techniques like VQ-VAE models have contributed to longer, more complex video generation, marking a shift in generation paradigms to include various data modalities [5].

## Integration of Multimodal Generation
The integration of multimodal systems has been revolutionized by innovations in large language models and structured optimization techniques [2]. The ease of incorporating varying input types into coherent output representations showcases the potential of models like EasyGen and DuoGen in generating multifaceted content effectively [6][7].

## Challenges and Open Problems
Despite these advancements, challenges such as maintaining temporal consistency in generated content, particularly in long video scenarios, persist [3][8]. Achieving a balance between computational efficiency and output quality will dictate the future directions in research for video synthesis and multimodal strategies [9].

## Trends and Open Problems
Active developments are noted in recent frameworks, highlighting the ongoing necessity to enhance multimodal understanding for longer video inputs and the utilization of advanced models to bridge the gaps between different modalities [10][3]. More comprehensive evaluations of generative models' performances are required to understand their capabilities better as they relate to human-centered implementations.
